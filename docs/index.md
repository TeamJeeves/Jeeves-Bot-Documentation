# Welcome To Jeeves

Jeeves is a Discord bot designed to be the ultimate companion for World of Warcraft players. 

With discord being the town square for most guilds and communities, Jeeves leverages its large database of game data and characters to bring better WoW integration to your server.

All the features have been made to either make Server/Raid Leaders lives a bit easier or to increase community engagement and fun in your Discord server.

Jeeves is built with input from top mythic raiders, collectors, and community organizers to provide an epic experience for your Discord community.  
Jeeves is able to scale from a small friends & family guild to a mega server. With countless configurations Jeeves is perfect for almost everyone.

# Jeeves's Uses

Many servers use jeeves. From the small Friends & Family Casual Guild, to the Hardcore Top World Raiding Guilds, all the way up to massive WoW Communities spanning thousands of players.

Jeeves can do alot, and you can configure its behavior to a way thats perfect for your specific server.

- News, Webhooks, Recruitment Feeds
- Server & Role Automation
- Officer & Managment Tools
- Keystone Tracking
- World Boss, Weekly Affixes, World Data Tracking
- Character Lookups
- Price Checks
- Fun & Games 

... and the list keeps growing.


## History
Jeeves was born in early 2017 under the name `The Lazy Peon`. It was a really bad bot. It broke constantly and was only for our guild's server, but it generated some amazing laughs. Not long after that `The Lazy Peon` was rebuilt and renamed `Jeeves`. Jeeves remained a staple in our guild's Discord for about a month before a friend randomly suggested to post it to Reddit to see if there would be any interest in the bot. After two hours and three upvotes, and no comments or installs we shrugged and moved on.

Little did we know what was in store for us when we woke up...  
We woke up to Jeeves in 100 servers, and many comments, questions, and feature requests on the reddit thread. Within an hour of waking up EU Support was added and many fires were put out. Since then its been a race to add more and more features, fix bugs and continouly improve Jeeves.

## The Future and Beyond
Jeeves is constantly being updated with new features. We have formed partnerships with various Fan Sites & Services like RaiderIO, TSM, WoWHead, WoWAudit to bring new and exciting features into your guilds discord. We firmly believe that Discord is here to stay and is the community platform for Warcraft. Our goal is to keep making it better by bringing Warcraft ever closer to discord itself.

Many of the features Jeeves has today originated from users bringing forth ideas or problems we could solve! 
Got an idea for something to be added to Jeeves? Add it to our suggestion tracker in the Jeeves Discord! Make sure to vote up ideas from others that matter to you!
